# Surface Brief: Swachha Parisara Civic Utility PWA

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- **Mode:** Operate (with Read secondary on `/learn` and `/learn/plastic-types`)
- **Target:** Whole application (`src/App.jsx` and all routes: `/home`, `/map`, `/report`, `/pickup`, `/rewards`, `/profile`, `/learn`, `/learn/plastic-types`, `/business`, `/auth`, `/privacy`, `/terms`)
- **Audience & Job:** Residents of Dakshina Kannada (primary) and local businesses, NGOs, and municipal officials (secondary) reporting plastic waste, scheduling collection, verifying drop-offs, and checking resin-code segregation outdoors on mobile or desktop.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Swachha Parisara is an authoritative coastal municipal waste & recovery ledger—calm, precise, and editorial like a well-run public transit or civic survey utility—refusing the category's childish eco-cartoon grass-green/school-bus-yellow clip-art, bubbly 24px pill cards, and curved-wave floating-button bottom bars.

OWN-WORLD: Warm Stone Paper ground (`#F7F7F5`), pure Alabaster data sheets (`#FFFFFF`), Cool Stone inset wells (`#EFEFEA`), Deep Basalt Ink (`#111513`), and Deep Estuary Pine (`#0F4C3A`) as the sole civic command accent, paired with Signal Ochre (`#D97706`) and Laterite Oxide (`#C2410C`) strictly for semantic state and heat-map severity. Typography pairs `Public Sans` with `Noto Sans Kannada` and `JetBrains Mono` for coordinates, resin codes, weights, and docket IDs. Raised by `crouwel-grid-specimen` (exposed 1px `#DDE0DC` hairline grid cells for metrics and ledgers), `vernacular-ephemera-jet-age-ticket-wallet` (structured docket rows with monospace reference codes and status stamps), and `notation-diagram-systems-orienteering-map` (cartographic legend and coordinate notation).

STORY: A resident or business operator opens the app and immediately trusts it as serious civic infrastructure: they inspect their verified recovery ledger or district waste map, file a geotagged report or schedule a pickup in under a minute, and track every collection through clear docket steps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top civic utility bar with the geometric meridian seal, "Swachha Parisara" wordmark, desktop navigation links, Ask assistant trigger, and `EN / ಕನ್ನಡ` switch; below it on `/home`, the resident's status row leads directly into the structured Municipal Recovery Ledger Card (large tabular kg readout on the left, etched line-and-hatch material recovery schematic on the right, and a 3-cell hairline-divided ledger footer below) paired with active collection dockets and the RIC `#1–#7` resin index strip; on mobile, a flat, crisp 5-tab architectural bottom dock with top active indicator bars anchors the viewport.
